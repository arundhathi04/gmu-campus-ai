# 🎓 GMU Campus AI

## Intelligent Campus Assistant for GM University

GMU Campus AI is a smart and interactive campus assistant designed to help students quickly access important information about GM University.

Students can ask questions about campus facilities, admissions, hostel services, transport, examinations, courses and more through a simple and modern interface.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🤖 AI Campus Assistant

Students can ask natural questions and receive campus-related answers.

Examples:

- Where is the library?
- Where is the admission office?
- What hostel facilities are available?
- Tell me about campus transport.
- Where is the examination office?

---

### 🎤 Voice Assistant

GMU Campus AI includes a voice assistant that allows students to ask questions using their voice.

The assistant listens when activated, processes the student's question and provides a spoken response.

It does not continuously speak or react to every interaction.

---

### 🏫 Campus Services

The application provides organized information about important campus services.

- 📚 Library
- 🏠 Hostel
- 🎓 Admissions
- 🚌 Transport
- 🍴 Food & Canteen
- 📝 Examinations
- 🏫 Campus

Each service opens its information directly within the same webpage.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of GMU Campus AI is to make university information easier and faster for students to access.

Instead of searching through different sources or asking multiple departments for basic information, students can interact with one centralized campus assistant.

> **One campus. One assistant. One place for information.**
